# Trace

**Understanding Microplastics. Taking Action.**

Trace is an AI-powered lifestyle and wellness platform concept that intercepts the
microplastics crisis at the consumer level — a digital lens making invisible threats in
daily water, food packaging, and clothing visible and manageable. Rather than another
guilt-driven eco-NGO, Trace is positioned as a premium biohacking tool: sleek, clinical,
and empowering.

## Brand strategy

Eco-conscious young adults in fast-paced urban environments need a transparent,
science-backed way to detect and reduce their daily exposure to microplastics — without
being paralyzed by the threat's invisibility, corporate greenwashing, or solutions that
don't fit a tight budget. Trace answers this for its primary persona, Shorouk (24,
Cairo): biologically fluent, visually driven, tech-reliant but skeptical of vague "green"
claims, and highly budget-conscious.

The brand's core tension — and its main design challenge — is staying premium and
scientific without becoming alarming: every warning is paired with a free or low-cost
action, and messaging leans on empowerment over guilt.

## Identity system

- **Logo** — a particle-core arch "A", developed through three rounds of concept
  exploration (microscope-in-wordmark, circular wave-and-magnifier, and the selected
  dissolved-particle arch)
- **Color** — a deep teal and aqua palette (`#0F4C5C`, `#2E6E7E`, `#6FA8A8`, `#A8C5C1`,
  `#DDEAE8`, `#F4FAFB`)
- **Typography** — Clash Display (Heavy) for headlines, Inter for UI and body copy
- **Voice** — four pillars: Understand, Research, Protect, Together

## Applications

Business card, mobile app (Learn → Scan → Take Action → Community), marketing website,
bilingual (English/Arabic) social media templates, and a merchandise line — all built as
one coherent system from the identity above.
